Privacy policy

DineroDPI for Android

DineroDPI is a self-custody wallet. Dinero Labs does not create an account for you and does not receive or store your recovery phrase or private keys.

Effective date: August 29, 2026
Application: DineroDPI for Android
Package: org.dinerolabs.dinerodpi

Information stored on your device

Your recovery phrase and private signing material remain encrypted on your Android device. Android backup is disabled for wallet application data. Optional embedded NodeCore synchronization data is stored in the app's no-backup directory and does not contain your recovery phrase or private keys.

Network information

To show balances, unspent outputs, and transaction history, the app sends derived public wallet addresses to configured Dinero RPC bridges. A bridge operator can observe the requesting IP address, queried public addresses, and signed transactions submitted for broadcast. The app compares independent bridge responses and does not send recovery phrases or private keys to a bridge.

If you enable the embedded stateless NodeCore, it connects directly to public Dinero peers. Those peers can observe a Dinero protocol connection from your device's public IP address.

Transactions

Transactions are constructed and signed locally. A signed raw transaction is sent to a bridge or peer network only after you authorize broadcasting. Blockchain transactions and public addresses are public by design and cannot be removed from the Dinero network by Dinero Labs.

Analytics, advertising, and sale of data

  • The app does not include advertising SDKs.
  • The app does not include third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Dinero Labs does not sell personal data collected through the app.

Security and your choices

You control the wallet and are responsible for keeping an offline recovery-phrase backup. Removing the wallet from the device deletes its local wallet data, but it cannot remove public blockchain records. Dinero Labs cannot recover a lost phrase, reverse a transaction, or access funds.

Children

DineroDPI is a financial utility and is not directed to children.
